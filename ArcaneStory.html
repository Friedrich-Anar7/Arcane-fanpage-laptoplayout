Synopsis

Piltover gleams on Hextech wealth while Zaun festers in the undercity beneath it, wo halves of the same city that stopped speaking the same language long ago. When a stolen invention places raw magic into untested hands, the fragile balance between them begins to collapse.

At the heart of the story are two sisters, Vi and Jinx, whose lives are torn apart by tragedy and shaped by the widening divide between Piltover and Zaun. As political ambitions, scientific progress, and personal loyalties collide, their fractured bond becomes the emotional core of the conflict.

Elsewhere, the partnership between Jayce and Viktor promises an age where science can change the world, only to reveal the cost of progress when ideals begin to diverge. Their work reshapes Piltover, influences the fate of Zaun, and draws governments, inventors, and revolutionaries into a struggle that extends far beyond the laboratory.

No life in Piltover or Zaun exists in isolation. Every invention changes more than technology, every alliance demands a sacrifice, and every act of love or vengeance leaves its mark on someone else. As old ideals crumble and new ones take their place, the story grows beyond any single hero or villain, becoming the collision of countless lives, each shaping the future in ways neither city could have imagined.

Below the Surface • The Undercity
Zaun
zaun

Beneath the shining skyline of Piltover lies Zaun, an industrial city forged from smoke, steel, and survival. Once part of the same city, Zaun became separated by decades of inequality, leaving its people to endure polluted air, hazardous factories, and a life defined by hardship rather than opportunity. Despite its reputation, Zaun is more than the undercity. It is a place where resilience thrives, where communities endure, and where necessity often gives rise to remarkable ingenuity.

Life in Zaun is shaped by contrasts. Inventors and laborers work beside chem-barons and smugglers, while children grow up learning that survival often depends on strength, loyalty, and sacrifice. The discovery of Shimmer, a powerful and dangerous substance capable of enhancing both body and mind, transforms the city, offering hope to some while deepening suffering for many others. As rival factions compete for influence, Zaun becomes a battleground where ideals, desperation, and ambition collide.

For generations, Zaun has lived beneath the shadow of Piltover, supplying its industry while receiving little of its prosperity. This imbalance fuels a growing desire for independence and recognition, inspiring figures who believe the undercity deserves to determine its own future. Throughout Arcane, Zaun is portrayed not simply as a place of conflict, but as a city filled with ordinary people striving to build meaningful lives despite overwhelming odds. It stands as a symbol of resilience, identity, and the enduring hope that even the darkest places can shape the future.

Shimmer

Shimmer

Arcane Zaun

Shimmer is a powerful chemical compound created in Zaun, capable of greatly enhancing physical strength, endurance, and healing. Its unstable nature, however, often comes at a devastating cost, altering both the body and mind of those who rely on it. Throughout Arcane, Shimmer stands as one of the undercity's most influential discoveries, blurring the line between scientific achievement and dangerous obsession.

Origins

Developed through the research of Singed and later exploited under Silco's rule, Shimmer became the foundation of Zaun's growing influence. What began as a scientific experiment evolved into a resource capable of changing the balance of power between Zaun and Piltover. Its production transformed the undercity's economy while strengthening those willing to embrace its risks.

Effects

The effects of Shimmer vary depending on its refinement and method of use. Some versions grant extraordinary physical abilities or rapid healing, while others produce violent mutations and unpredictable behavior. Its versatility allows it to function as medicine, weapon, performance enhancer, and industrial resource, making it one of the most valuable substances in Zaun.

Role in Arcane

Beyond its physical effects, Shimmer symbolizes the difficult choices facing Zaun. For many, it offers the only path toward survival or strength in a city long neglected by Piltover. Yet every advancement comes with consequences, reflecting one of Arcane's central themes: progress achieved without restraint often demands a human cost.

Fire Lights

The Firelights

Arcane Zaun

The Firelights are a group of Zaunites led by Ekko, formed to protect the undercity from the growing influence of Shimmer and those who exploit it. Operating from a hidden sanctuary, they provide safety for displaced citizens while resisting the violence that has consumed much of Zaun. Their symbol, a glowing firelight, reflects their belief that hope can survive even in the darkest places.

Foundation

The group emerged as Zaun descended further into conflict under Silco's rule. Witnessing the destruction caused by Shimmer and the suffering of innocent people, Ekko gathered those who shared his vision of rebuilding rather than controlling the undercity. Their refuge became a place where families, children, and inventors could live beyond the reach of fear.

Philosophy

Unlike many factions driven by power or revenge, the Firelights believe that Zaun's future depends on cooperation, creativity, and compassion. They fight only when necessary, protecting their community while refusing to abandon the hope that the undercity can become something greater than the conflict surrounding it.

Role in Arcane

Throughout Arcane, the Firelights stand as a counterbalance to both Piltover's neglect and Silco's methods. They demonstrate that strength can be found not only through force, but through unity, resilience, and the determination to build a better future.

The Rebellion

The Rebellion

Arcane Zaun

Long before the main events of Arcane, Zaun's people rose against Piltover in pursuit of equality, freedom, and recognition. Years of exploitation and neglect had created deep resentment within the undercity, leading many to believe that independence could only be achieved through resistance. The uprising would become one of the defining moments in the history of both cities.

Causes

While Piltover prospered through trade and innovation, Zaun remained burdened by dangerous industry, poverty, and political exclusion. The growing divide between the two cities left many Zaunites convinced that peaceful change was no longer possible, setting the stage for open conflict.

The Uprising

The rebellion ultimately ended in tragedy, claiming countless lives and leaving lasting scars on everyone involved. Among those forever changed was Vander, whose experiences during the conflict shaped his later commitment to peace and protecting the people of the Lanes from another devastating war.

Legacy

Although the uprising failed, its ideals never disappeared. The desire for an independent Zaun continued to inspire future generations, influencing leaders, revolutionaries, and ordinary citizens alike. Throughout Arcane, the memory of the rebellion remains a powerful reminder of both the cost of freedom and the consequences of division.

Hextech powers advanced tools, transportation systems, mining equipment, and defensive technology. Act OneSeason I — The Spark

Synopsis

Season One begins in the aftermath of Zaun's failed uprising against Piltover, where the divide between the prosperous city above and the struggling undercity below continues to deepen. Raised under Vander's protection, Vi and Powder grow up believing that family is their greatest strength. However, a simple robbery in Piltover unexpectedly sets a chain of events into motion, drawing inventors, politicians, criminals, and ordinary citizens into a conflict that will reshape both cities. What begins as a desperate attempt to survive soon becomes the catalyst for a much greater story.

In Piltover, Jayce and Viktor achieve a scientific breakthrough by successfully combining magic with technology, giving birth to Hextech and opening the door to a new era of innovation. Their discovery promises prosperity not only for Piltover but for the wider world, earning admiration and political influence. Yet every advancement brings new responsibilities, and as Hextech grows more powerful, questions arise over who should control it and how far its potential should be allowed to reach.

Meanwhile, Zaun follows a very different path. Silco strengthens his influence through the development of Shimmer, believing that only power can secure the independence his people have long been denied. His rise transforms the undercity, creating new opportunities for some while leaving others trapped in an increasingly violent struggle for survival. As old loyalties are tested and new ambitions emerge, the gap between Piltover and Zaun becomes wider than ever.

At the heart of the season lies the transformation of its characters. Vi and Powder are separated by tragedy, their bond fractured by guilt, loss, and years of misunderstanding. Jayce and Viktor begin as partners united by a common vision but gradually discover that even shared ideals can lead to different futures. Across both cities, friendships evolve into rivalries, alliances are forged and broken, and every decision leaves lasting consequences that extend far beyond those who make them.

Throughout the season, Arcane explores the relationship between progress and sacrifice, asking whether remarkable achievements can ever be separated from the cost they demand. By its conclusion, the futures of Piltover and Zaun have become inseparably linked, while a single, devastating act leaves both cities standing on the edge of a conflict that neither side can control. Season One closes not with resolution, but with the promise that every choice made so far will shape the world still to come.

Act ThreeSeason II — The Reckoning

Synopsis

Season Two opens in the immediate aftermath of Jinx's attack on Piltover's Council, an event that shatters any remaining hope for peaceful reconciliation between Piltover and Zaun. With much of the city's leadership lost and trust completely broken, Piltover prepares for war while Zaun struggles to define its future following Silco's death. What began as political rivalry evolves into open conflict, forcing every major character to confront the consequences of choices made long before the first shot is fired. As both cities descend further into chaos, familiar relationships are pushed to their limits. Vi and Caitlyn fight to protect Piltover while searching for a path that avoids repeating the mistakes of the past. Jinx, now viewed by many as both a revolutionary symbol and a dangerous criminal, continues to wrestle with the fractured identity left behind by Powder. Their complicated bond remains the emotional heart of the story, demonstrating that even amidst war, family can never be easily forgotten.

Meanwhile, Jayce and Viktor pursue increasingly different visions for the future of Hextech. Once united by the dream of improving lives through science, they gradually become divided by their beliefs about progress and humanity itself. Viktor's pursuit of perfection leads him beyond the limits of ordinary invention, transforming Hextech from a technological breakthrough into a force capable of reshaping life itself. As the Arcane grows increasingly unstable, scientific ambition becomes inseparable from moral responsibility. The conflict also expands beyond Piltover and Zaun with the arrival of Ambessa Medarda and the growing influence of Noxus. Seeking to exploit the instability for political and military advantage, Ambessa introduces new alliances, powerful armies, and broader ambitions that threaten to turn a local conflict into one with consequences reaching far beyond the two cities. At the same time, long-buried experiments resurface through Warwick, whose tragic existence becomes another reminder of the devastating cost of unchecked ambition.

Throughout the season, Arcane explores themes of redemption, identity, sacrifice, and the limits of progress. Former enemies are forced into uneasy alliances, old friendships are tested beyond repair, and every victory demands a painful price. Rather than offering clear heroes or villains, the story continues to challenge each character's beliefs, revealing that compassion and understanding often require greater courage than violence or revenge. By its conclusion, the conflict between Piltover and Zaun reaches its final chapter as the Arcane itself threatens both cities with destruction. Jayce and Viktor make extraordinary sacrifices to prevent catastrophe, Jinx finally breaks free from the identity that has defined her for so long, and the survivors are left to rebuild a future shaped by loss, forgiveness, and hope. Season Two closes the story not with triumph or defeat, but with the understanding that true progress is measured not by power or invention, but by the choices people make to create a better world.

Legacy— What Endures Beyond the Ashes

Reflection

Legacy is not measured by power, victory, or even survival. It is measured by the influence of those who shaped history long after they are gone. History remembers the names of leaders, visionaries, revolutionaries, and conquerors. As the people of Piltover and Zaun look beyond the scars of the past, they inherit more than a rebuilt world; they inherit the responsibility to shape one worthy of those who came before. For beyond every battle, every invention, and every sacrifice, one truth endures beyond the ashes: hope.
